LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Nagercoil between:

LESSOR: Chatura Chada, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Yutika Bahri, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Rajasthan.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 328/B, admeasuring 1.29 acres, classified as Agricultural land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 20 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹890,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹128,000 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before November of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for horticultural plantation; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor confirms that the land described herein, Survey No. 328/B, is unencumbered and free from any third-party claims.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Nagercoil.

8. JURISDICTION
The courts at Nagercoil shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
