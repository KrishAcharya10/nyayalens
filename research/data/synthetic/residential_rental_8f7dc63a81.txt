RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Ahmedabad between:

LANDLORD: Imaran Mitra, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Harinakshi Din, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Telangana.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 12 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Lessee/Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of ₹59,500 to the Lessor/Landlord on or before the 5th day of each calendar month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹178,500 to the Landlord prior to taking possession.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
A notice of 90 days shall be required from either party intending to terminate this tenancy.

5. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Ahmedabad.

7. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Ahmedabad alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
