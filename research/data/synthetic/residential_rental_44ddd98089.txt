RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Ahmedabad between:

LANDLORD: Frederick Iyengar, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Suhani Ram, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Delhi.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 36 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Lessee/Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of ₹63,500 to the Lessor/Landlord on or before the 5th day of each calendar month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Security deposit of ₹381,000 shall be paid at the time of execution of this Agreement and refunded within 30 days of vacating the premises.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
A notice of 90 days shall be required from either party intending to terminate this tenancy.

5. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance of the premises, while structural repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

8. JURISDICTION
The courts at Ahmedabad shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
