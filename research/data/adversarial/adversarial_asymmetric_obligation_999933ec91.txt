LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Pune between:

LESSOR: Avi Narayan, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Jeevika Dhar, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Uttar Pradesh.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 104/B, admeasuring 22 guntha, classified as Agricultural land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 5 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹3,570,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹255,000 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before May of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for cultivation of seasonal crops; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. NOTICE PERIOD - LESSOR
The Lessor may terminate this Deed by serving a notice period of 15 days in writing to the Lessee.

6. NOTICE PERIOD - LESSEE
The Lessee may terminate this Deed by serving a notice period of 60 days in writing to the Lessor.

7. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Pune.

9. JURISDICTION
The courts at Pune shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
