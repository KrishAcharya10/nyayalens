RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Chennai between:

LANDLORD: Yamini Grover, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Saumya Hayre, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Madhya Pradesh.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 60 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
Monthly rent payable under this Agreement shall be ₹61,500, due on or before the 1st of every month, payable via bank transfer.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
A sum of ₹615,000 shall be deposited by the Lessee with the Lessor as security, refundable upon vacation subject to deductions for damages.

4. MAINTENANCE
Day-to-day upkeep of the premises shall be the responsibility of the Tenant; major structural repairs shall be undertaken by the Landlord.

5. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Chennai.

7. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Chennai alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
