RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Jaipur between:

LANDLORD: Liam Sahota, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Wishi Chacko, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Telangana.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 24 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Tenant agrees to remit a sum of ₹45,500 per month towards rent, such payment to be made no later than the 5th of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Security deposit of ₹273,000 shall be paid at the time of execution of this Agreement and refunded within 30 days of vacating the premises.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 90 days' written notice to the other party.

5. MAINTENANCE
Day-to-day upkeep of the premises shall be the responsibility of the Tenant; major structural repairs shall be undertaken by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Jaipur.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Jaipur alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
