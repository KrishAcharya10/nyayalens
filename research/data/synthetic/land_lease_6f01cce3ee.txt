LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Patna between:

LESSOR: Daksh Dada, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Hemal Baria, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Karnataka.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 910/2, admeasuring 8.76 acres, classified as Agricultural land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 5 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹1,370,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
In addition to the lease premium, the Lessee shall pay an annual lease rent of ₹119,500 payable on or before January each year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The Lessee shall use the leased land solely for the purpose of construction of a warehouse facility and for no other purpose without prior written consent.

5. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

6. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Patna alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
