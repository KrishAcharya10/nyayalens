LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Lucknow between:

LESSOR: Lucky Anand, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Dev Panchal, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Tamil Nadu.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 686/A, admeasuring 1219 sq. ft., classified as Non-Agricultural (NA) land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 20 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹3,530,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
In addition to the lease premium, the Lessee shall pay an annual lease rent of ₹144,500 payable on or before October each year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for horticultural plantation; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor hereby declares that the said land bearing Survey No. 686/A is free from all encumbrances, charges, and liens.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Lucknow alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
