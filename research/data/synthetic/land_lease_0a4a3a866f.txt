LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Pune between:

LESSOR: Siya Chahal, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Baghyawati Thaman, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Gujarat.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 563/B, admeasuring 17030 sq. ft., classified as Agricultural land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 30 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹510,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
In addition to the lease premium, the Lessee shall pay an annual lease rent of ₹128,000 payable on or before October each year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The Lessee shall use the leased land solely for the purpose of agricultural storage purposes and for no other purpose without prior written consent.

5. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

7. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Pune alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
