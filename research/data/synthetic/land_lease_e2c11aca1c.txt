LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Kolkata between:

LESSOR: Upadhriti Shroff, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Adweta Chowdhury, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of West Bengal.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 685/2, admeasuring 13455 sq. ft., classified as Commercial land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 10 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹4,940,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹109,500 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before May of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The Lessee shall use the leased land solely for the purpose of cultivation of seasonal crops and for no other purpose without prior written consent.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor confirms that the land described herein, Survey No. 685/2, is unencumbered and free from any third-party claims.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Kolkata.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Kolkata alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
