RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Jaipur between:

LANDLORD: Ekavir Nagar, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Champak Gokhale, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Karnataka.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 11 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Lessee/Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of ₹54,500 to the Lessor/Landlord on or before the 1st day of each calendar month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹545,000 to the Landlord prior to taking possession.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
This Agreement may be terminated by either party upon serving a notice period of 15 days in writing.

5. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

6. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Jaipur alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
