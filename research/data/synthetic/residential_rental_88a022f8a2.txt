RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Ahmedabad between:

LANDLORD: Anusha Sastry, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Arunima Dixit, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Telangana.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 24 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Tenant agrees to remit a sum of ₹77,500 per month towards rent, such payment to be made no later than the 10th of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹232,500 to the Landlord prior to taking possession.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
A notice of 90 days shall be required from either party intending to terminate this tenancy.

5. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance of the premises, while structural repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. JURISDICTION
The courts at Ahmedabad shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
