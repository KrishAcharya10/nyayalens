LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Kanpur between:

LESSOR: Rishi Luthra, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Chandresh Banik, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Rajasthan.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 479/2, admeasuring 19291 sq. ft., classified as Residential Conversion Pending land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 15 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹3,800,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹30,000 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before April of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for agricultural storage purposes; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor hereby declares that the said land bearing Survey No. 479/2 is free from all encumbrances, charges, and liens.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Kanpur.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Kanpur alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
