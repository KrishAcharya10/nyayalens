RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Nagercoil between:

LANDLORD: Saanvi Acharya, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Girish Pathak, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Madhya Pradesh.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 60 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
Monthly rent payable under this Agreement shall be ₹19,000, due on or before the 1st of every month, payable via bank transfer.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹190,000 to the Landlord prior to taking possession.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 60 days' written notice to the other party.

5. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Nagercoil.

7. JURISDICTION
The courts at Nagercoil shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
