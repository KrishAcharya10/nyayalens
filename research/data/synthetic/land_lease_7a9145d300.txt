LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Lucknow between:

LESSOR: Lucky Mangat, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Gautam Subramaniam, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Telangana.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 119/B, admeasuring 9.28 acres, classified as Non-Agricultural (NA) land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 20 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹640,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹138,500 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before January of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The Lessee shall use the leased land solely for the purpose of agricultural storage purposes and for no other purpose without prior written consent.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor confirms that the land described herein, Survey No. 119/B, is unencumbered and free from any third-party claims.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. JURISDICTION
The courts at Lucknow shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
