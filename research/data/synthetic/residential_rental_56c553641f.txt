RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Lucknow between:

LANDLORD: Manthan Thaman, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Raagini Shere, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Uttar Pradesh.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 60 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Lessee/Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of ₹20,500 to the Lessor/Landlord on or before the 7th day of each calendar month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹123,000 to the Landlord prior to taking possession.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
This Agreement may be terminated by either party upon serving a notice period of 30 days in writing.

5. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance of the premises, while structural repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Lucknow alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
