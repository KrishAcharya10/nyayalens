LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Thane between:

LESSOR: Chatura Mangat, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Tristan Mandal, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Delhi.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 484/A, admeasuring 3.36 hectares, classified as Residential Conversion Pending land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 30 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹2,580,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹291,000 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before November of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for agricultural storage purposes; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor hereby declares that the said land bearing Survey No. 484/A is free from all encumbrances, charges, and liens.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Thane.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Thane alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
