LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Vadodara between:

LESSOR: Ridhi Narayanan, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Isaiah Nadig, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Maharashtra.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 275/A, admeasuring 5.11 acres, classified as Agricultural land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 20 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹820,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹120,000 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before August of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The Lessee shall use the leased land solely for the purpose of construction of a warehouse facility and for no other purpose without prior written consent.

5. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Vadodara.

7. JURISDICTION
The courts at Vadodara shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
