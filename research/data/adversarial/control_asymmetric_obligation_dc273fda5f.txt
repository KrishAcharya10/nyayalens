LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Kolkata between:

LESSOR: Hiral Prabhakar, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Christopher Walia, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Madhya Pradesh.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 681/1, admeasuring 10 guntha, classified as Agricultural land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 5 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹3,770,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹265,000 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before August of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for agricultural storage purposes; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. NOTICE PERIOD - LESSOR
The Lessor may terminate this Deed by serving a notice period of 30 days in writing to the Lessee.

6. NOTICE PERIOD - LESSEE
The Lessee may terminate this Deed by serving a notice period of 30 days in writing to the Lessor.

7. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

9. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Kolkata alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
