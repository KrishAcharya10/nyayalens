RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Nashik between:

LANDLORD: Saumya Roy, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Anusha Bhandari, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Gujarat.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 24 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Tenant agrees to remit a sum of ₹17,000 per month towards rent, such payment to be made no later than the 7th of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹170,000 to the Landlord prior to taking possession.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 90 days' written notice to the other party.

5. MAINTENANCE
Day-to-day upkeep of the premises shall be the responsibility of the Tenant; major structural repairs shall be undertaken by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Nashik.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Nashik alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
