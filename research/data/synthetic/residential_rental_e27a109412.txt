RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Nagercoil between:

LANDLORD: Netra Suri, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Baghyawati Tata, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Telangana.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 24 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Tenant agrees to remit a sum of ₹60,500 per month towards rent, such payment to be made no later than the 10th of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Security deposit of ₹363,000 shall be paid at the time of execution of this Agreement and refunded within 30 days of vacating the premises.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
A notice of 60 days shall be required from either party intending to terminate this tenancy.

5. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Nagercoil.

7. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Nagercoil alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
