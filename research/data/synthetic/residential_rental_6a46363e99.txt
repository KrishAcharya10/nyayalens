RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Bengaluru between:

LANDLORD: Frederick Saran, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Sarthak Chatterjee, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Tamil Nadu.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 12 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Tenant agrees to remit a sum of ₹39,000 per month towards rent, such payment to be made no later than the 1st of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Security deposit of ₹78,000 shall be paid at the time of execution of this Agreement and refunded within 30 days of vacating the premises.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
A notice of 45 days shall be required from either party intending to terminate this tenancy.

5. MAINTENANCE
Day-to-day upkeep of the premises shall be the responsibility of the Tenant; major structural repairs shall be undertaken by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Bengaluru alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
