LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Chennai between:

LESSOR: Yochana Rajagopal, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Rachit Atwal, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of West Bengal.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 552/1, admeasuring 5424 sq. ft., classified as Residential Conversion Pending land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 20 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹1,770,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹101,500 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before June of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The Lessee shall use the leased land solely for the purpose of horticultural plantation and for no other purpose without prior written consent.

5. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Chennai.

7. JURISDICTION
The courts at Chennai shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
