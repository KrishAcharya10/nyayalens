RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Hyderabad between:

LANDLORD: Orinder Manne, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Ayaan Thaker, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Maharashtra.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 36 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
Rent for the demised premises is fixed at ₹25,500 per calendar month, payable in advance by the 5th of the month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹76,500 to the Landlord prior to taking possession.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days' written notice to the other party.

5. MAINTENANCE
Day-to-day upkeep of the premises shall be the responsibility of the Tenant; major structural repairs shall be undertaken by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Hyderabad alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
