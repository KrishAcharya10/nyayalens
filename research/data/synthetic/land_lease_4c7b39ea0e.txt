LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Surat between:

LESSOR: Vedhika Narayanan, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Yuvraj Upadhyay, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Delhi.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 864/3, admeasuring 8.42 acres, classified as Commercial land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 20 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹1,070,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹201,000 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before December of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for agricultural storage purposes; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Surat.

7. JURISDICTION
The courts at Surat shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
