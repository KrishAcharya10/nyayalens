LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Ahmedabad between:

LESSOR: Aadhya Raj, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Yashica Keer, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of West Bengal.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 971/3, admeasuring 0.33 hectares, classified as Agricultural land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 20 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹4,040,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹288,500 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before June of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for commercial retail development; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Ahmedabad.

7. JURISDICTION
The courts at Ahmedabad shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
