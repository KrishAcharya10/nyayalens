LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Bhopal between:

LESSOR: Faras Radhakrishnan, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Bhanumati Nair, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Bihar.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 389/1, admeasuring 11713 sq. ft., classified as Residential Conversion Pending land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 20 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹1,230,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
In addition to the lease premium, the Lessee shall pay an annual lease rent of ₹193,000 payable on or before November each year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for dairy farming operations; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor confirms that the land described herein, Survey No. 389/1, is unencumbered and free from any third-party claims.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. JURISDICTION
The courts at Bhopal shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
