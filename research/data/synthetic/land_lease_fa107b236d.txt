LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Delhi between:

LESSOR: Jonathan Yohannan, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Chaaya Sundaram, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Rajasthan.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 333/A, admeasuring 35 guntha, classified as Agricultural land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 5 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹980,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹93,500 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before September of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for agricultural storage purposes; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor confirms that the land described herein, Survey No. 333/A, is unencumbered and free from any third-party claims.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Delhi.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Delhi alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
