RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Kolkata between:

LANDLORD: Vanya Sunder, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Unni Bhatt, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Gujarat.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 24 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Tenant agrees to remit a sum of ₹65,500 per month towards rent, such payment to be made no later than the 7th of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
A sum of ₹131,000 shall be deposited by the Lessee with the Lessor as security, refundable upon vacation subject to deductions for damages.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
A notice of 15 days shall be required from either party intending to terminate this tenancy.

5. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance of the premises, while structural repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Kolkata alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
