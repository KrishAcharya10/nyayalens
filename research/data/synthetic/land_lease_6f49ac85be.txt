LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Nagercoil between:

LESSOR: Liam Divan, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Liam Zacharia, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of West Bengal.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 68/A, admeasuring 15188 sq. ft., classified as Agricultural land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 15 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹3,580,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹203,500 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before January of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for cultivation of seasonal crops; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor confirms that the land described herein, Survey No. 68/A, is unencumbered and free from any third-party claims.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Nagercoil.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Nagercoil alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
