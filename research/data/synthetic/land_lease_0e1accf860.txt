LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Nagercoil between:

LESSOR: Dakshesh Goswami, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Neelima Sheth, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Tamil Nadu.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 143/1, admeasuring 2.03 hectares, classified as Residential Conversion Pending land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 20 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹3,250,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹157,500 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before August of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The Lessee shall use the leased land solely for the purpose of horticultural plantation and for no other purpose without prior written consent.

5. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Nagercoil.

7. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Nagercoil alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
