LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Surat between:

LESSOR: Kabir Wason, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Ekani Chaudhuri, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Madhya Pradesh.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 284/3, admeasuring 6.93 acres, classified as Commercial land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 20 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹490,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
In addition to the lease premium, the Lessee shall pay an annual lease rent of ₹101,500 payable on or before March each year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The Lessee shall use the leased land solely for the purpose of dairy farming operations and for no other purpose without prior written consent.

5. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

7. JURISDICTION
The courts at Surat shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
