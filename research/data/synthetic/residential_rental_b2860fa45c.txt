RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Thane between:

LANDLORD: Madhav Parikh, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Aryan Dey, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Karnataka.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 36 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
Rent for the demised premises is fixed at ₹23,500 per calendar month, payable in advance by the 1st of the month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Security deposit of ₹47,000 shall be paid at the time of execution of this Agreement and refunded within 30 days of vacating the premises.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 15 days' written notice to the other party.

5. MAINTENANCE
Day-to-day upkeep of the premises shall be the responsibility of the Tenant; major structural repairs shall be undertaken by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

8. JURISDICTION
The courts at Thane shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
