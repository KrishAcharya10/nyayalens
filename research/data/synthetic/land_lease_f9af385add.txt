LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Mumbai between:

LESSOR: Wridesh Pandey, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Kevin Nigam, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Delhi.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 843/3, admeasuring 9.91 acres, classified as Non-Agricultural (NA) land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 15 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹520,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹51,500 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before March of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The Lessee shall use the leased land solely for the purpose of horticultural plantation and for no other purpose without prior written consent.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor hereby declares that the said land bearing Survey No. 843/3 is free from all encumbrances, charges, and liens.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Mumbai.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Mumbai alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
