LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Nagpur between:

LESSOR: Jagrati Majumdar, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Gavin Yohannan, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of West Bengal.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 185/B, admeasuring 3.29 hectares, classified as Non-Agricultural (NA) land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 10 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹3,810,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹64,500 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before September of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The Lessee shall use the leased land solely for the purpose of horticultural plantation and for no other purpose without prior written consent.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor hereby declares that the said land bearing Survey No. 185/B is free from all encumbrances, charges, and liens.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

8. JURISDICTION
The courts at Nagpur shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
