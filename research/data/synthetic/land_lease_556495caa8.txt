LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Mumbai between:

LESSOR: Andrew Sinha, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Diya Kade, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Maharashtra.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 925/B, admeasuring 28 guntha, classified as Residential Conversion Pending land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 15 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹3,060,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
In addition to the lease premium, the Lessee shall pay an annual lease rent of ₹194,000 payable on or before March each year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for dairy farming operations; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Mumbai.

7. JURISDICTION
The courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
