RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Bengaluru between:

LANDLORD: David Prashad, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Bishakha Patel, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Bihar.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 24 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Tenant agrees to remit a sum of ₹56,500 per month towards rent, such payment to be made no later than the 7th of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹660,000 to the Landlord prior to taking possession, refundable within 30 days of vacating the premises subject to an itemized list of deductions not exceeding 10% of the deposit.

4. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance of the premises, while structural repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

5. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

7. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Bengaluru alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
