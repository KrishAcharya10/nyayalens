LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Mumbai between:

LESSOR: Janaki Shere, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Anita Acharya, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Maharashtra.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 949/B, admeasuring 2922 sq. ft., classified as Non-Agricultural (NA) land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 15 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹4,820,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
In addition to the lease premium, the Lessee shall pay an annual lease rent of ₹56,500 payable on or before January each year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for construction of a warehouse facility; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor confirms that the land described herein, Survey No. 949/B, is unencumbered and free from any third-party claims.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

8. JURISDICTION
The courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
