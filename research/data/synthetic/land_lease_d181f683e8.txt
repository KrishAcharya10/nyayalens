LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Nashik between:

LESSOR: Karan Balan, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Udyati Banik, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Delhi.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 275/A, admeasuring 9.3 acres, classified as Non-Agricultural (NA) land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 10 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹1,130,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
In addition to the lease premium, the Lessee shall pay an annual lease rent of ₹262,000 payable on or before August each year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The Lessee shall use the leased land solely for the purpose of dairy farming operations and for no other purpose without prior written consent.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor confirms that the land described herein, Survey No. 275/A, is unencumbered and free from any third-party claims.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Nashik.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Nashik alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
