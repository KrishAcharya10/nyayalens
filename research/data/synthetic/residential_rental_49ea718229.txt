RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Vadodara between:

LANDLORD: Shaurya Ganesh, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Noah Ramaswamy, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Maharashtra.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 60 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
Rent for the demised premises is fixed at ₹69,000 per calendar month, payable in advance by the 1st of the month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
A sum of ₹414,000 shall be deposited by the Lessee with the Lessor as security, refundable upon vacation subject to deductions for damages.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 15 days' written notice to the other party.

5. MAINTENANCE
Day-to-day upkeep of the premises shall be the responsibility of the Tenant; major structural repairs shall be undertaken by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Vadodara.

8. JURISDICTION
The courts at Vadodara shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
