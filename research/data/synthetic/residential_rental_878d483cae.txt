RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Pune between:

LANDLORD: Rudra Goswami, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Widisha Lalla, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Rajasthan.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 12 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
Monthly rent payable under this Agreement shall be ₹45,500, due on or before the 5th of every month, payable via bank transfer.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹136,500 to the Landlord prior to taking possession.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
A notice of 30 days shall be required from either party intending to terminate this tenancy.

5. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance of the premises, while structural repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Pune.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Pune alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
