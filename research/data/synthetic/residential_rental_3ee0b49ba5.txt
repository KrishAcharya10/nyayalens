RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Vadodara between:

LANDLORD: Anvi Sane, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Kamya Gara, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Madhya Pradesh.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 60 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
Rent for the demised premises is fixed at ₹61,000 per calendar month, payable in advance by the 7th of the month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Security deposit of ₹366,000 shall be paid at the time of execution of this Agreement and refunded within 30 days of vacating the premises.

4. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance of the premises, while structural repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

5. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Vadodara.

7. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Vadodara alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
