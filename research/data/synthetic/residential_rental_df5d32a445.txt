RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Bhopal between:

LANDLORD: Aahana Cheema, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Jeevika Rana, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Delhi.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 36 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
Monthly rent payable under this Agreement shall be ₹49,000, due on or before the 10th of every month, payable via bank transfer.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Security deposit of ₹294,000 shall be paid at the time of execution of this Agreement and refunded within 30 days of vacating the premises.

4. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

5. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

6. JURISDICTION
The courts at Bhopal shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
