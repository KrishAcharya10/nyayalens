RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Bengaluru between:

LANDLORD: Veda Batta, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Shravya Issac, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Telangana.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 36 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
Rent for the demised premises is fixed at ₹26,500 per calendar month, payable in advance by the 5th of the month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹159,000 to the Landlord prior to taking possession.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
This Agreement may be terminated by either party upon serving a notice period of 30 days in writing.

5. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance of the premises, while structural repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Bengaluru.

8. JURISDICTION
The courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
