LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Nagpur between:

LESSOR: Robert Shankar, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Radhika Yogi, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Delhi.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 251/3, admeasuring 2090 sq. ft., classified as Commercial land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 15 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹1,810,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹249,500 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before July of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for construction of a warehouse facility; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor confirms that the land described herein, Survey No. 251/3, is unencumbered and free from any third-party claims.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Nagpur alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
