LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Nagercoil between:

LESSOR: Mohini Kunda, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Unni Biswas, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Telangana.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 816/2, admeasuring 39 guntha, classified as Agricultural land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 5 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹1,110,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹295,500 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before February of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for construction of a warehouse facility; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor confirms that the land described herein, Survey No. 816/2, is unencumbered and free from any third-party claims.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

8. JURISDICTION
The courts at Nagercoil shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
