RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Bhopal between:

LANDLORD: Ubika Sood, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Ranbir Choudhury, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Karnataka.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 36 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
Monthly rent payable under this Agreement shall be ₹21,500, due on or before the 7th of every month, payable via bank transfer.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
A sum of ₹43,000 shall be deposited by the Lessee with the Lessor as security, refundable upon vacation subject to deductions for damages.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 15 days' written notice to the other party.

5. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance of the premises, while structural repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. JURISDICTION
The courts at Bhopal shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
