RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Bengaluru between:

LANDLORD: Maanas Deol, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Avni Banerjee, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Bihar.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 12 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Lessee/Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of ₹59,000 to the Lessor/Landlord on or before the 7th day of each calendar month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹177,000 to the Landlord prior to taking possession.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 15 days' written notice to the other party.

5. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance of the premises, while structural repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Bengaluru.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Bengaluru alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
