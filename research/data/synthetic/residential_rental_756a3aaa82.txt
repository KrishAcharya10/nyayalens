RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Mumbai between:

LANDLORD: George Wali, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Agastya Borah, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of West Bengal.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 12 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Lessee/Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of ₹36,500 to the Lessor/Landlord on or before the 5th day of each calendar month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
A sum of ₹365,000 shall be deposited by the Lessee with the Lessor as security, refundable upon vacation subject to deductions for damages.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
A notice of 60 days shall be required from either party intending to terminate this tenancy.

5. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance of the premises, while structural repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Mumbai.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Mumbai alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
