LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Thane between:

LESSOR: Vasudha Mutti, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Vaishnavi Johal, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Telangana.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 687/2, admeasuring 5228 sq. ft., classified as Commercial land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 30 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
An upfront lease premium amounting to ₹2,650,000 shall be payable by the Lessee prior to handover of possession of the land.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
Annual rent of ₹198,500 for the leased land shall be paid by the Lessee to the Lessor on or before March of every calendar year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The Lessee shall use the leased land solely for the purpose of cultivation of seasonal crops and for no other purpose without prior written consent.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor confirms that the land described herein, Survey No. 687/2, is unencumbered and free from any third-party claims.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Thane alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
