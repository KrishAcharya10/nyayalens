RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Nagpur between:

LANDLORD: Peter Aurora, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Kashvi Patla, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Delhi.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 11 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Tenant agrees to remit a sum of ₹45,000 per month towards rent, such payment to be made no later than the 10th of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹94,000 to the Landlord prior to taking possession, refundable within 30 days of vacating the premises subject to an itemized list of deductions not exceeding 10% of the deposit.

4. NOTICE PERIOD - LANDLORD
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement by serving a notice period of 30 days in writing to the Tenant.

5. NOTICE PERIOD - TENANT
The Tenant may terminate this Agreement by serving a notice period of 30 days in writing to the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Nagpur.

8. JURISDICTION
The courts at Nagpur shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
