RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Delhi between:

LANDLORD: Chasmum Mand, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Aarnav Khurana, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Maharashtra.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 24 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Lessee/Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of ₹76,500 to the Lessor/Landlord on or before the 5th day of each calendar month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
A sum of ₹459,000 shall be deposited by the Lessee with the Lessor as security, refundable upon vacation subject to deductions for damages.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 90 days' written notice to the other party.

5. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

6. JURISDICTION
The courts at Delhi shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
