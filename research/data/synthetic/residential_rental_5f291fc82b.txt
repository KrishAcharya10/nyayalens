RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Jaipur between:

LANDLORD: Oliver Bahl, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Bina Sura, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Tamil Nadu.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 24 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Tenant agrees to remit a sum of ₹11,000 per month towards rent, such payment to be made no later than the 7th of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Security deposit of ₹33,000 shall be paid at the time of execution of this Agreement and refunded within 30 days of vacating the premises.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days' written notice to the other party.

5. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance of the premises, while structural repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
In case of breach of any material term of this Agreement, the aggrieved party may terminate the Agreement after serving due notice.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

8. JURISDICTION
The courts at Jaipur shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
