LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Surat between:

LESSOR: Urmi Mishra, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Unni Krish, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Bihar.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 244/A, admeasuring 16337 sq. ft., classified as Non-Agricultural (NA) land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 5 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹4,140,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
In addition to the lease premium, the Lessee shall pay an annual lease rent of ₹267,000 payable on or before January each year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The land hereby leased shall be utilised exclusively for construction of a warehouse facility; any change in use shall require the Lessor's written approval.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor confirms that the land described herein, Survey No. 244/A, is unencumbered and free from any third-party claims.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Surat.

8. JURISDICTION
The courts at Surat shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
