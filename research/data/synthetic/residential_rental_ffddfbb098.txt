RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Kolkata between:

LANDLORD: Faras Kadakia, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Gavin Sankaran, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Uttar Pradesh.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 60 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
The Tenant agrees to remit a sum of ₹48,000 per month towards rent, such payment to be made no later than the 5th of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
A sum of ₹288,000 shall be deposited by the Lessee with the Lessor as security, refundable upon vacation subject to deductions for damages.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days' written notice to the other party.

5. MAINTENANCE
Day-to-day upkeep of the premises shall be the responsibility of the Tenant; major structural repairs shall be undertaken by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

8. JURISDICTION
The courts at Kolkata shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
