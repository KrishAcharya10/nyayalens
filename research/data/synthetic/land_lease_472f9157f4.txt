LAND LEASE DEED

This Deed of Lease is executed at Patna between:

LESSOR: Jai Bose, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessor',

AND

LESSEE: Orinder Kala, hereinafter referred to as the 'Lessee'.

This Deed is governed by the laws of Uttar Pradesh.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the land bearing Survey No. 705/A, admeasuring 3218 sq. ft., classified as Commercial land, and has agreed to lease the same to the Lessee on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Lease shall remain in force for a period of 15 years from the date of execution hereof.

2. LEASE PREMIUM
The Lessee shall pay a one-time lease premium of ₹1,640,000 to the Lessor upon execution of this Deed.

3. ANNUAL LEASE RENT
In addition to the lease premium, the Lessee shall pay an annual lease rent of ₹77,500 payable on or before October each year.

4. PERMITTED USE
The Lessee shall use the leased land solely for the purpose of dairy farming operations and for no other purpose without prior written consent.

5. ENCUMBRANCE DECLARATION
The Lessor confirms that the land described herein, Survey No. 705/A, is unencumbered and free from any third-party claims.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

8. JURISDICTION
The courts at Patna shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising from this Agreement.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Deed on the date first above written.
