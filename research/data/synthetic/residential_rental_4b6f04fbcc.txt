RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Delhi between:

LANDLORD: Nirja Sachdev, hereinafter referred to as the 'Landlord',

AND

TENANT: Urmi Sawhney, hereinafter referred to as the 'Tenant'.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Bihar.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the residential premises situated at the above address, and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following terms:

1. LEASE DURATION
This Agreement shall remain valid for a period of 36 months commencing from the date of execution hereof.

2. RENT PAYMENT
Rent for the demised premises is fixed at ₹10,500 per calendar month, payable in advance by the 7th of the month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹31,500 to the Landlord prior to taking possession.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
This Agreement may be terminated by either party upon serving a notice period of 90 days in writing.

5. MAINTENANCE
Day-to-day upkeep of the premises shall be the responsibility of the Tenant; major structural repairs shall be undertaken by the Landlord.

6. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement forthwith in the event of non-payment of rent for two consecutive months.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably, failing which the matter shall be subject to arbitration in Delhi.

8. JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Delhi alone shall have jurisdiction.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first above written.
